The officials want your bar closed. Their muscle just walked in. Four rounds: punks, boxers, a bat, a cook with a sack of potatoes, and finally Rocco & Ivan, the bouncers who think the bar is theirs.

Controls
Quest 3
Swing for real: speed sets damage
Forearms in front of face: block
Duck or lean: dodge a glowing fist
Hit the swinging arm: parry
Grip: bottle, stool or a collar
Release mid-swing: throw
Squeeze grip as a potato arrives: catch it
Swat the bat mid-swing: disarm
Bottle to mouth: drink
Left stick move · right stick snap turn
A: restart after a win
Left X: perf + tuning panel (left stick tunes)
Desktop
Mouse look · WASD move
Left / right click (or J / K): punch
Shift: heavy · hold X: light
Space: block · C: duck · G: headbutt
E: grab / drop / catch · Q: throw
F: drink a held bottle · R: restart
P: perf panel · [ ] pick · − = tune
